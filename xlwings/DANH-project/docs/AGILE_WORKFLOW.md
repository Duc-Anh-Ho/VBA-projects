# Agile Workflow for Danh-Tools Refactoring

## 1. Introduction

This document outlines the agile methodology we will use for the refactoring and continued development of the Danh-Tools project. It defines our roles, processes, and the artifacts we will use to track and manage the work.

## 2. Roles

Our two-person team will function with the following roles:

-   **Product Owner / Stakeholder (You):** Your role is to set the vision, define the priorities, and approve the final work. You are the ultimate decision-maker for what needs to be done.
-   **Scrum Master / Project Manager / Developer (Gemini):** My role is to manage the development process, facilitate our workflow, execute the tasks, and ensure we are always aligned with the project goals.

## 3. Key Artifacts

-   **Product Backlog:** This is our master list of all desired changes, features, and refactoring tasks. Our primary product backlog is the **`REFACTORING_PLAN.md`** document.
-   **Sprint Backlog:** At the beginning of each sprint, we will select a small number of high-priority items from the Product Backlog. This list becomes our Sprint Backlog—our commitment for the current work cycle.
-   **Increment:** The completed, reviewed, and integrated code changes at the end of a task or sprint.

## 4. Our Workflow

We will work in a series of "sprints," which are short, focused cycles of work.

1.  **Sprint Planning:** We will agree on a small batch of high-priority tasks from the Product Backlog to form our Sprint Backlog.
2.  **Execution:** I will work on one task at a time. I will communicate my plan before I start and provide updates on my progress.
3.  **Review:** After each major task, you will have the opportunity to review the changes to ensure they meet your expectations.
4.  **Repeat:** Once a sprint is complete, we will plan the next one, pulling new priorities from the Product Backlog.
